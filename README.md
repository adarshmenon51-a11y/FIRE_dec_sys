# IoT-Based Petrol Pump Fire Safety System

## Overview

An IoT-based fire and safety monitoring system designed to improve safety at petrol stations by detecting potential fire hazards and hazardous conditions. The system monitors fire, carbon monoxide (CO), and vehicle entry into designated safety zones.

When a vehicle enters the monitored area, the system activates its safety monitoring process. If fire or a hazardous gas condition is detected, the system automatically triggers the extinguisher mechanism and generates alerts to notify motorists and emergency responders.

The project aims to enhance fire prevention, reduce response time, and improve safety in fuel dispensing environments through automated monitoring and emergency response.

## Features

- **Fire Detection:** Detects fire conditions within the monitored petrol station area.
- **Carbon Monoxide Monitoring:** Monitors CO levels to identify potentially hazardous conditions.
- **Vehicle Detection:** Detects vehicles entering the designated safety zone.
- **Automatic Extinguisher Activation:** Triggers the extinguisher mechanism when fire or hazardous gas conditions are detected.
- **Real-Time Alerts:** Provides alerts to motorists and emergency responders during hazardous situations.
- **Automated Safety Monitoring:** Integrates sensors and control mechanisms to improve safety monitoring.

## System Workflow

1. **Vehicle Detection:** Detects a vehicle entering the designated petrol station safety zone.
2. **System Activation:** Activates the safety monitoring process.
3. **Hazard Monitoring:** Monitors fire and carbon monoxide levels within the designated area.
4. **Hazard Detection:** Identifies potentially dangerous conditions based on sensor readings.
5. **Emergency Response:** Activates the automatic extinguisher mechanism when a fire or hazardous gas condition is detected.
6. **Alert Generation:** Notifies motorists and emergency responders of the detected hazard.
